## Featured Analytics Projects

These projects demonstrate practical work across **financial risk, e-commerce behaviour, supply chain operations, and customer analytics**. Each repository includes its business problem, methodology, tools, findings, and limitations.

### 1. Credit Card Fraud Detection & Risk Analysis
**Python · MySQL · Power Query · Power BI**

- Analysed a synthetic dataset of **100,000 transactions**, including **2,055 fraud-labelled transactions**.
- Investigated fraud patterns by transaction channel, time band, country risk flag, category, and transaction velocity.
- Reported a **2.055% fraud-label share** and compared segment-level fraud rates to identify areas for further investigation.
- Built a multi-page analytical dashboard and documented data-quality checks, class imbalance, and target-leakage risks.

[View Repository](https://github.com/Vikant07/credit-card-fraud-detection-risk-analysis)

### 2. E-Commerce Shopper Behaviour & Cart Abandonment
**Python · SQL · Power BI**

- Explored approximately **one million synthetic shopper profiles** to examine purchase behaviour, cart abandonment, and customer segments.
- Analysed conversion and abandonment indicators across customer, product, and device dimensions.
- Developed a three-page Power BI report covering executive overview, shopper segmentation, and device/product analysis.
- Documented the key limitation: the profile-level dataset is not event-level clickstream data, so it cannot establish an actual session funnel or abandoned-cart revenue.

[View Repository](https://github.com/Vikant07/-E-Commerce-Shopper-Behavior-Cart-Abandonment)

### 3. Supply Chain Delivery Performance Dashboard
**Python · SQL · SQLite · Power BI**

- Analysed **99,441 Olist e-commerce orders** using nine cleaned relational tables.
- Measured an **8.11% late-delivery rate** across 96,470 eligible delivered orders.
- Compared average review scores for on-time/early deliveries (**4.29/5**) and late deliveries (**2.57/5**).
- Built order-level and order-item-level analytical views to control join grain and avoid inflated counts and freight metrics.
- Examined regional fulfilment variation, freight economics, and seller concentration.

[View Repository](https://github.com/Vikant07/supply-chain-delivery-performance-dashboard)

### 4. Bank Customer Churn Analysis
**Python · SQL · Power BI · Statistical Analysis**

- Analysed **10,000 customer records** and measured a historical churn rate of **20.38%**.
- Identified segment differences, including **32.44% churn in Germany** and **56.04% in the 50–59 age group**.
- Compared churn rates for inactive (**26.87%**) and active customers (**14.27%**).
- Applied Chi-square and Mann–Whitney U tests to examine statistical associations.
- Documented small-sample cautions, unknown complaint timing, and other limitations before drawing conclusions.

[View Repository](https://github.com/Vikant07/Customer-Churn-Analysis-for-a-Bank)
